# VitaDent product context

## Confirmed
- Clinic/brand: VitaDent — Inteligencia Odontológica.
- Scope now: frontend prototype in Django Templates; no React or Vite.
- Public visitors do not create accounts. They request appointments through a conversational AI chatbot; WhatsApp is a separate direct-contact channel.
- Chatbot technology planned: Rasa, with intent recognition, entity extraction, and contextual dialogue tracking.
- First delivery includes public clinic pages, a reception/admin interface, and a professional view prototype.
- Administration will have its own authenticated account. Each specialist will have an individual account and can see only patients assigned to that specialist.
- The user supplied a clinic manual. It names nine specialists, clinic hours, treatment groups, and some indicative appointment durations.
- Do not display prices from the manual.
- The supplied logo uses cyan and lime on black; the web design uses a light background for legibility.
- Pearl is the public website reference; SRTdash is the administrative UI reference.

## Unknown; keep configurable and visibly pending
- Official WhatsApp number, location/address, email, clinic photos, social links, and appointment confirmation policy.
- Actual appointment inventory and live clinic data.

## Prototype boundary
- Static sample content is demonstrative, not a real booking system or Rasa connection.
- No patient login/account creation, working staff authentication, authorization filtering, payment, or backend persistence in this frontend stage.
- Do not invent testimonials, credentials, availability, addresses, or prices.
