# VitaDent web interface direction

- Public mode: guide visitors to request a dental appointment through the Rasa conversational assistant. Direct WhatsApp contact is separate.
- Admin mode: help reception review appointment requests and maintain specialist/treatment information.
- Specialist mode: show an individual professional only their assigned patients after authentication.
- Public site: Pearl's Bootstrap layout, typography, navigation, card patterns, and dental-service presentation, adapted to VitaDent's real content and Django Templates.
- Admin panel: SRTdash's sidebar, compact navigation, dashboard, and table patterns, adapted to reception work in Django Templates.
- Brand: cyan and lime from the provided mark, supported by deep ink and warm off-white. Present the logo on a light surface with its dark field removed.
- Use quiet, spacious public sections; use a compact, scannable operations layout in the admin.
- The public booking action opens the Rasa chatbot UI; visitors do not create patient accounts.
- Avoid prices, fake patient accounts, fabricated clinic facts, and testimonials. Template stock photography is labelled illustrative and does not stand in for VitaDent staff or premises.
